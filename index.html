Privacy Policy for ScanFlow

Effective Date: September 28, 2025
Last Updated: September 28, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how ScanFlow ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Documents and Images: Photos and documents you capture or import using the app
  • Text Content: Text extracted from your documents through OCR processing
  • App Settings: Your preferences and configuration settings

Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Usage Analytics: How you interact with the app (anonymized)
  • Crash Reports: Technical information to help us fix bugs and improve performance

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide OCR Services: Process your documents and extract text
  • Store Your Documents: Save scanned documents locally on your device
  • Improve App Performance: Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Fix Technical Issues: Use crash reports to identify and resolve bugs
  • Provide Customer Support: Assist you with app-related questions

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • All your documents and extracted text are stored locally on your device
  • We do not upload your documents or text to our servers
  • Your data remains under your complete control

Security Measures

  • Data is stored securely using device encryption
  • We implement industry-standard security practices
  • Regular security updates and improvements

Data Sharing

We DO NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your documents or extracted text with anyone
  • Upload your content to external servers
  • Use your documents for advertising purposes

We MAY share anonymized, aggregated data for:

  • App improvement and analytics
  • Crash reporting and bug fixes
  • Performance optimization

Third-Party Services

Our app may use the following third-party services:

  • Google ML Kit: For text recognition (processing happens on-device)
  • Firebase Analytics: For app usage analytics (anonymized data only)
  • Firebase Crashlytics: For crash reporting and performance monitoring

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all data stored by the app
  • Delete: Remove any documents or data from the app
  • Export: Share your documents using the app's export features
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics in app settings

Permissions We Request

Camera Permission

  • Purpose: To capture photos of documents for OCR processing
  • Usage: Only when you actively use the camera feature
  • Storage: Images are processed and stored locally on your device

Storage Permission

  • Purpose: To save scanned documents and import existing images
  • Usage: Only for documents you choose to save or import
  • Access: Limited to app-specific folders and user-selected files

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.
